# Farkle Scoring Rules

## Basic Scoring

### Individual Dice
- **1** = 100 pts
- **5** = 50 pts

### Three of a Kind
- Three 1s = 300 pts
- Three 2s = 200 pts
- Three 3s = 300 pts
- Three 4s = 400 pts
- Three 5s = 500 pts
- Three 6s = 600 pts

### Multiple of Same Number
- **Four of any number** = 1,000 pts
- **Five of any number** = 2,000 pts
- **Six of any number** = 3,000 pts

### Special Combinations
- **1-6 Straight** (1,2,3,4,5,6) = 1,500 pts
- **Three Pairs** (e.g., 1,1,2,2,3,3) = 1,500 pts
- **Four-of-a-kind + Pair** (e.g., 1,1,1,1,2,2) = 1,500 pts
- **Two Triplets** (e.g., 1,1,1,2,2,2) = 2,500 pts

## Game Rules

1. **Goal**: First to reach **10,000 points**
2. **Final Round**: When someone reaches 10k, everyone else gets ONE more turn
3. **Farkle**: If you roll no scoring dice, you lose all points from that turn
4. **Hot Dice**: If you use all 6 dice, you get to roll all 6 again
5. **Banking**: You can choose to "Bank" your points and end your turn safely

## How to Play

### Starting the Game
1. Someone types `/play` to create a lobby
2. Others click **Join Game** button
3. Host clicks **Start Game** when ready

### Taking Your Turn
1. Roll shows your dice (1️⃣2️⃣3️⃣4️⃣5️⃣6️⃣)
2. Click numbered buttons (①②③...) to select dice to keep
3. Selected dice show ✅
4. Click **🔄 Roll Again** to score selected dice and roll remaining
5. Click **💰 Bank** to save your points and end turn

### Display
- **Leaderboard**: Shows all players' scores (▶️ = current player)
- **Turn Message**: Shows current roll and selection buttons
- Both update automatically as game progresses

### Commands
- `/play` - Create or join a game
- `/stop` - End game early and show final results
- `/start` - Bot welcome message
